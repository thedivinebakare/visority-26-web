## Meet the Speakers
**Section Header**: Experts at the Forefront of Visibility and Authority

### Divine Chukwuemeka
*Migration Researcher, TEDx Speaker, Founder, EmigrateEase*  
Divine Mmachukwu Chukwuemeka is a Migration Researcher, TEDx speaker, and founder working at the intersection of global mobility, youth development, and opportunity access.  
She holds a First-Class Bachelor’s degree in History and International Studies from Anchor University and currently serves as a Graduate Research Assistant at CEPDeR, Covenant University, where she contributes to research on migration and development.  
She is the founder of Linked On LinkedIn and EmigrateEase, which helps professionals build visibility and approach global mobility with clarity.  
She has spoken on diverse platforms, including TEDx at Anchor University. Beyond research and speaking, Divine is deeply committed to youth impact, equipping young people to access global opportunities.  
She is a convener and community builder, leading initiatives such as Beyond Borders and the AheadGen Mentorship Program, where she creates spaces for meaningful dialogue and mentorship.  
Through her digital presence, she engages a growing audience with insights on migration, career growth, and visibility.

### Mandy Chinedum
*Creative Director, Founder, AmpliQ*  
Mandy Chinedum is an Entrepreneur and Creative Director.  
Over the years, he has led the transformation of more than 50 corporate brands across diverse industries, delivering creative and strategic solutions that improve positioning, customer perception, and long-term business performance. He has collaborated with organisations such as Bukz Accounting UK, Plivra US, Davanni Realty, Hypriest Media, Haimpress Canada, Mudres, Maxivize Limited, Jamie Pajoel International, Jawo Africa, ModelCare Medicals UK, among others.  
As the Founder of AmpliQ, Africa’s leading freelancers’ operations ecosystem, Mandy is building the infrastructure that empowers freelancers and creative entrepreneurs to operate sustainable, scalable businesses. He is also the host of FreelancersOS, one of Africa’s largest freelancer-focused business events powered by AmpliQ Meet. Through its debut live session on X, the platform reached over 55,000 live viewers, equipping freelancers with practical systems for business operations and sustainable growth.  
He is an awardee of the 100 Under 40 African Creative Entrepreneurs 2026.
