Where is Visority ’26 happening?+

Visority ’26 is fully online. Join from wherever you are. The team will share joining instructions with registered attendees before the conference.

When do the sessions start?+

28, 29 and 30 October 2026, starting at 8pm West Africa Time (WAT, UTC+1) each night.

Is the Standard pass really free?+

Yes. Standard includes all three live sessions and networking. Premium costs ₦5,000 and adds recordings, session notes, the premium community, priority Q&A, the resource vault and an identity tag.

Will I be able to watch the recordings?+

Recordings and session notes are included in the Premium pass, so you can revisit the conversations after the event.

Who is this experience for?+

Founders, creatives, digital entrepreneurs, professionals and emerging leaders who want to turn their work into visibility, commercial value and trusted authority.

How will I receive access details?+

Provide your email and WhatsApp number when you register so the team can share event updates and joining instructions. If you need help, email nextgencon01@gmail.com.
