3-Day MasterClass to Position Yourself as the Authority in Your Field

Visority '26 was born from the two words — Visibility and Authority — and from a burden to build the next generation of global innovators and builders from Africa.

It brings together founders, creatives, freelancers, digital entrepreneurs, professionals, and young leaders from diverse backgrounds to move from the industry's best-kept secret to authority.

Visority '26 goes beyond a virtual event; it is a room where creatives get the mind recalibration to move past fear and build innovative ideas with audacity.
