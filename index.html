● ABOUT VISORITY '26

3-DAY MASTERCLASS TO POSITION YOURSELF AS THE AUTHORITY IN YOUR FIELD

Visority '26 was born from the two words – Visibility and Authority and from a burden to build the next generation of global innovators and builders from Africa.

It brings together founders, creatives, freelancers, digital entrepreneurs, professionals, and young leaders from diverse backgrounds to move from the industry's best-kept secret to authority.

Visority '26 goes beyond a virtual event; it is a room where creatives get the mind recalibration to move past fear and build innovative ideas with audacity.

What Is Visority '26?

+
Visority '26 is a virtual conference hosted by NextGen, bringing together creatives, founders, and digital entrepreneurs to become visible, be an authority in their field and become part of a thriving community to accelerate their growth.

When Is Visority ‘26 Happening?

+
Visority ‘26 is a virtual event and will be happening live on Google Meet from 28th- 30th October, 2026.

Is Attendance Virtual Or In-Person?

+
Visority ‘26 is a fully virtual event that promises to be immersive and interactive, with sessions from experts and industry leaders.

Is This The First Edition?

+
This is the second edition of the Next Gen Conference. The first edition of the NextGen Conference was held in 2024 with over 500 attendees across 14 countries.

What Is The Daily Schedule For The Event?

+
Each session will last for two hours, from 8:00 pm to 10:00 pm WAT.

How Do I Prepare Ahead for Visority '26

+
First step is to get your tickets. Once your seat is secured, follow us on our social media page to stay updated on key event details. Engage with keynote speakers ahead of the event to be familiar with them and prepare your questions on areas you need clarity.
