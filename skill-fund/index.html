WHAT THE FUND PAYS FOR

Three things money
can actually buy.

◐

Pass subsidies

We sponsor Premium passes for builders whose talent is ready but whose budget is not. The Standard pass stays free for everyone; the fund exists for the people who would otherwise be priced out of the full experience.

◑

Tools & software

Production software, subscriptions and infrastructure credits, so a promising builder is not held back by a tool budget on the day they finally get seen.

◒

1-on-1 mentorship

Time with verified industry experts. Not a stage, not a panel — a working conversation with someone who has already solved the problem in front of them.

WHO IT IS FOR

Built for people
the budget
quietly excluded.

The fund is deliberately narrow. It is not a scholarship programme and it is not marketing. It targets a specific kind of gap, and it is funded by partners who want that gap closed.

  • Emerging African buildersFounders, creatives, digital entrepreneurs and young professionals building serious work, often while carrying the cost of it themselves.
  • Ready, not polishedYou do not need a finished brand or a polished story. The fund targets people with the capability and the intent, not people who already look ready.
  • Invisible by circumstanceTalented people who stay overlooked for structural reasons — money, access, proximity — rather than a lack of output.
HOW IT WORKS

Apply, get matched,
then get to work.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you build

    A short application so we understand your work and where a pass, a tool or a conversation would help most.

  2. 02

    We match the support

    The fund decides whether the most useful support is a subsidised pass, a tools grant, or mentorship. We do not ask everyone for the same thing.

  3. 03

    You attend and build

    The funded pass covers the full three-night experience, so the only question left is whether you show up.
